# XPlanung24 QGIS Plugin

This plugin enables you to:
- display zoning, land-use, and regional plans in compliance with the **XPlanung** standard,  
- translate and visualize spatial data, regulations, and textual sections,  
- securely manage and collaboratively access planning documents via the **XPlanung24** platform.  

---

## What is XPlanung24

**XPlanung24** is a web-based platform by [Bau.Land.XPlan eGbR](https://www.xplanung24.de) for the centralized management, visualization, and collaborative editing of municipal zoning, land-use, and regional plans in the **XPlanung** format.  

The platform offers features such as:
- upload, validation, and visualization of **XPlanGML** files,  
- publication and approval workflows.  

The development of XPlanung24 is funded by the **State of North Rhine-Westphalia**.

---

## What is XPlanung

**XPlanung** is a nationwide standard for the digital exchange of zoning, land-use, and spatial planning documents in Germany.  
It is based on **XML/GML (XPlanGML)** and ensures that planning information can be exchanged between software systems without geometric or semantic loss.  

The standard was officially adopted by the **IT Planning Council** on **October 5, 2017**, and is continuously maintained and developed by the **Coordination Office XPlanung/XBau**.
